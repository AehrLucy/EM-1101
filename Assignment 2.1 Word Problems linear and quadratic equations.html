Calculus & Optimization Readiness

Translating complex physical constraints into precise mathematical models is the cornerstone of higher mathematics. Linear rate relationships $\frac{dx}{dt}$ directly underpin Related Rates in Differential Calculus. Quadratic vertex models preview finding global extrema $f'(x) = 0$ in Optimization, while kinematic height functions $h(t) = -\frac{1}{2}gt^2 + v_0 t + h_0$ form the bedrock of Integral Kinematics. Pay careful attention to defining variable domains and rejecting non-viable roots.

🧠 Modeling & Solution Strategies

Core mathematical frameworks, algebraic techniques, and analytical guidelines.

Linear Models & Rate Balances

First-Order
  • Uniform Motion: $d = r \cdot t \implies t = \frac{d}{r}$. Always express total time as the sum of interval times: $t_{\text{total}} = t_1 + t_2 + \dots + t_n$.
  • Work-Rate Equations: If a system completes a unit job in time $T$, its rate is $r = \frac{1}{T}$. Combined rates add linearly: $\frac{1}{T_1} + \frac{1}{T_2} = \frac{1}{T_{\text{combined}}}$.
  • Mixture Continuity: Amount of solute remains invariant: $C_1 V_1 + C_2 V_2 = C_f (V_1 + V_2)$, where $C$ is concentration percentage and $V$ is volume.

Quadratic Equations & Optimization

Second-Order

Standard Form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \quad (a \neq 0)$

  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Discriminant Analysis ($\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$): If $\Delta > 0$, two real solutions exist; if $\Delta = 0$, one distinct solution; if $\Delta < 0$, solutions are non-real complex numbers.
  • Parabolic Vertex (Optimization): For $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$, the extreme value occurs at the axis of symmetry $x_v = -\frac{b}{2a}$. Maximum if $a < 0$, minimum if $a > 0$.

⚡ Critical Problem-Solving Guidelines

1. Extraneous & Physical Root Rejection: Quadratic equations algebraically yield two roots. You must evaluate solutions against physical constraints (e.g., $t > 0$, $d > 0$, positive dimension quantities $x > 0$).
2. Geometric & Vector Relations: Perpendicular motion generates right-triangle relationships governed by $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Relative motion against current or wind modifies velocity: $v_{\text{eff}} = v_{\text{still}} \pm v_{\text{medium}}$.

PART I Linear Equation Applications (20 Exercises)

First-Order Modeling & Rate Systems
Problem 01 • Dilution & Mixture

A chemist mixes Solution $A$ ($40\%$ acid concentration) and Solution $B$ ($70\%$ acid concentration) to produce a $90\text{ L}$ solution. However, during transfer, an additional $6\text{ L}$ of pure water is accidentally introduced into the mixture. How many liters of Solution $A$ and Solution $B$ must be combined initially so that the final total liquid ($96\text{ L}$) achieves exactly a $50\%$ overall acid concentration?

Problem 02 • Multi-Segment Kinematics

An express train completes a $450\text{ km}$ journey in exactly $t_{\text{total}} = 5\text{ hours}$. In urban zones, it travels at an average speed of $v_1 = 60\text{ km/h}$. On open track, it accelerates to $v_2 = 110\text{ km/h}$. A signal delay forces it to crawl at $v_3 = 30\text{ km/h}$ for $30\text{ minutes}$ ($0.5\text{ h}$). Calculate the distance traveled by the train on the open track segment.

Problem 03 • Linear Age Relations

Four years ago, Arthur's age $A - 4$ was three times Beatrice's age $B - 4$. In eight years, twice Arthur's age $2(A + 8)$ will exceed three times Beatrice's age $3(B + 8)$ by exactly $14\text{ years}$. Determine Arthur's current age $A$.

Problem 04 • Blend Pricing & Margin

A coffee merchant blends Arabica beans valued at $\$14/\text{lb}$ with Robusta beans at $\$8/\text{lb}$ to produce a $100\text{ lb}$ specialty batch. If the merchant sets the final retail selling price at $\$15/\text{lb}$ to realize a $25\%$ profit margin over unit production cost, how many pounds of Arabica beans are in the blend?

Problem 05 • Variable Efficiency Work

Master technician Alex can overhaul an engine in $6\text{ hours}$ (rate $r_A = \frac{1}{6}$). Apprentice Blake takes $10\text{ hours}$ ($r_B = \frac{1}{10}$). Alex begins alone at 8:00 AM. At 10:00 AM, Blake joins, but Alex's efficiency drops to $80\%$ ($0.8r_A$) due to instructing Blake. At what exact time will the engine overhaul be completed?

Problem 06 • Triangulation Geometry

In $\triangle ABC$, angle measure $\theta_2 = 3\theta_1 - 10^\circ$. The third angle satisfies $\theta_3 = (\theta_1 + \theta_2) + 20^\circ$. Utilizing the Euclidean angle sum identity $\sum \theta_i = 180^\circ$, compute the measure of the smallest angle in the triangle.

Problem 07 • Intercept Motion

A courier departs a facility on a motorcycle at $v_m = 45\text{ mph}$. $40\text{ minutes}$ ($t_0 = \frac{2}{3}\text{ h}$) later, a pursuit vehicle leaves the facility along the same path at $v_c = 65\text{ mph}$, but loses $10\text{ minutes}$ ($\frac{1}{6}\text{ h}$) at a checkpoint. How far from the facility does the pursuit vehicle overtake the courier?

Problem 08 • Portfolio Allocation

An investment fund divides $\$50,000$ across three accounts yielding simple annual interest rates of $4\%$, $6\%$, and $9\%$. The principal invested at $9\%$ is twice the principal at $4\%$. If total annual interest yield is $I = \$3,420$, calculate the principal allocated to the $6\%$ account.

Problem 09 • Positional Digit Puzzle

A two-digit integer $N = 10t + u$ has a tens digit $t = u + 3$. Reversing the digits yields $N_{\text{rev}} = 10u + t = N - 27$. Given that $N + N_{\text{rev}} = 121$, find the original integer $N$.

Problem 10 • Flow Rate & Drainage

Inlet Pipe $A$ fills a reservoir in $12\text{ h}$ ($r_A = \frac{1}{12}$), Pipe $B$ fills it in $15\text{ h}$ ($r_B = \frac{1}{15}$), and Drain Pipe $C$ empties it in $20\text{ h}$ ($r_C = -\frac{1}{20}$). Pipes $A$ and $B$ operate together for $3\text{ hours}$, after which $C$ is also opened. How many additional hours are required to completely fill the reservoir?

Problem 11 • Vector Hydrodynamics

A boat travels $d = 48\text{ miles}$ downstream in $t_{\text{down}} = 3\text{ h}$ ($v_b + v_c = \frac{48}{3}$) and returns upstream in $t_{\text{up}} = 4\text{ h}$ ($v_b - v_c = \frac{48}{4}$). Find the current velocity $v_c$ and the time required for a unpowered raft to drift $12\text{ miles}$ powered solely by $v_c$.

Problem 12 • Geometric Invariance

A rectangular field has perimeter $P = 2(L + W) = 240\text{ m}$. If length is adjusted to $L' = L - 10$ and width to $W' = W + 6$, the area remains invariant ($L \cdot W = L' \cdot W'$). Determine the original dimensions $L$ and $W$.

Problem 13 • Metallurgical Alloy Fusion

Alloy $X$ consists of $70\%$ copper and $30\%$ zinc. Alloy $Y$ contains $40\%$ copper and $60\%$ zinc. Determine the mass of Alloy $X$ ($m_X$) and Alloy $Y$ ($m_Y$) required to formulate $150\text{ kg}$ of a new alloy containing exactly $50\%$ copper.

Problem 14 • Multi-Tier Revenue System

A hall sells $N = 800$ total tickets across VIP ($p_1 = \$120$), Orchestra ($p_2 = \$80$), and Balcony ($p_3 = \$45$). Revenue from Orchestra seats equals total combined revenue from VIP and Balcony seats ($80 N_2 = 120 N_1 + 45 N_3$). Given total revenue $R = \$64,000$, calculate Balcony tickets sold ($N_3$).

Problem 15 • Piecewise Wage Model

A consultant earns base rate $w\text{ (\$/h)}$ for the first $30\text{ hours}$ in a week, and overtime rate $1.5w$ for hours exceeding $30$. Working $44\text{ hours}$ in a week with a flat travel stipend of $\$150$, total gross pay was $\$3,370$. Solve for base rate $w$.

Problem 16 • Oscillatory Distance Integration

Two cyclists separated by $D = 105\text{ miles}$ travel toward each other. Cyclist $A$ rides at $v_A = 16\text{ mph}$. Cyclist $B$ departs $0.5\text{ h}$ later at $v_B = 20\text{ mph}$. At the instant $B$ departs, a bird leaves $B$ at speed $v_p = 30\text{ mph}$, flying continuously back and forth between $A$ and $B$ until they meet. Compute total distance $d_{\text{bird}}$ flown by the bird.

Problem 17 • Corporate Budget Allocation

An initial budget $B$ allocates $60\%$ to R&D, $25\%$ to Marketing, and the remaining $\$450,000$ to Operations ($0.15B = 450,000$). Next quarter, total budget increases by $20\%$ ($B' = 1.20B$), with R&D taking $65\%$ and Marketing taking $20\%$. Calculate the new dollar amount allocated to Operations ($15\%\text{ of } B'$).

Problem 18 • Consecutive Odd Integers

Let $x, x+2, x+4$ represent three consecutive positive odd integers. Given that four times the sum of the first and third integers exceeds five times the second integer by $33$: $$4(x + [x+4]) - 5(x+2) = 33$$ Find the three integers.

Problem 19 • Per-Capita Cost Shift

A group of $n$ students split a total charter cost of $C = \$1,200$ equally. When $4$ students withdraw, the per-student cost increases by $\$10$: $$\frac{1200}{n - 4} - \frac{1200}{n} = 10$$ Determine original group size $n$.

Problem 20 • Three-Component Concentration

A beverage maker combines Pure Juice ($0\%$ sugar), Nectar ($12\%$ sugar), and Concentrate ($60\%$ sugar) to formulate $500\text{ L}$ of a $18\%$ sugar mixture. If volume of Pure Juice used ($V_1$) is twice the Concentrate volume ($V_3$), calculate required Nectar volume ($V_2$).

PART II Quadratic Equation Applications (20 Exercises)

Second-Order Systems, Kinematics & Extrema
Problem 21 • Kinematic Trajectory

A projectile is launched from height $h_0 = 60\text{ m}$ with initial velocity $v_0 = 28\text{ m/s}$. Height in meters above ground is modeled by: $$h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 28t + 60$$ Determine the exact timestamp $t > 0$ when the projectile reaches $h(t) = 80\text{ m}$ on its downward trajectory.

Problem 22 • Parabolic Revenue Model

At base ticket price $p = \$50$, attendance is $N = 1,200$. For every $\$2$ price increase ($p(x) = 50 + 2x$), attendance drops by $30$ people ($N(x) = 1200 - 30x$). Total revenue is $R(x) = p(x) \cdot N(x)$. Find optimal price $p$ and maximum revenue $R_{\max}$.

Problem 23 • Right Triangle Geometry

In a right triangle, hypotenuse $c = x + 4$, longer leg $b = x$, and shorter leg $a = x - 4$. Applying $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$: $$(x - 4)^2 + x^2 = (x + 4)^2$$ Solve for $x$ and determine all three side lengths.

Problem 24 • Combined Work Rational Model

Two pipes fill a pool in $8\text{ hours}$ together. Operating alone, the smaller pipe requires $12\text{ hours}$ longer than the larger pipe ($T_s = T_l + 12$). $$\frac{1}{T_l} + \frac{1}{T_l + 12} = \frac{1}{8}$$ Solve the resulting quadratic equation for larger pipe time $T_l$.

Problem 25 • Non-Linear Speed Difference

A express train covers $d = 300\text{ miles}$ at speed $v$. A high-speed bullet train travels at $v + 15\text{ mph}$ and completes the distance in $1\text{ hour}$ less time: $$\frac{300}{v} - \frac{300}{v + 15} = 1$$ Calculate standard express speed $v$.

Problem 26 • Uniform Border Area

A pool measuring $20\text{ m} \times 12\text{ m}$ is surrounded by a walkway of uniform width $x\text{ m}$. Combined total area is $A_{\text{total}} = 525\text{ m}^2$: $$(20 + 2x)(12 + 2x) = 525$$ Determine border width $x$.

Problem 27 • Product of Even Integers

The product of two consecutive positive even integers $2k$ and $2k+2$ is $224$ less than $5$ times their sum: $$(2k)(2k + 2) = 5(4k + 2) - 224$$ Solve for the two integers.

Problem 28 • Multi-Divider Boundary Optimization

A farmer has $160\text{ m}$ of fencing to enclose a rectangular pasture along a river (no fence along river). A dividing fence perpendicular to the river splits it into two equal sections. Total fencing $3x + y = 160 \implies y = 160 - 3x$. Maximize area $A(x) = x(160 - 3x)$.

Problem 29 • Diagonal Geometry

A rectangle has diagonal $D = 25\text{ in}$. Length $L = W + 17$. Utilizing $W^2 + (W + 17)^2 = 25^2$, calculate width $W$, length $L$, perimeter $P$, and area $A$.

Problem 30 • Commercial Profit & Loss

A merchant purchases $N$ units for $\$1,800$ (cost per unit $c = \frac{1800}{N}$). $6$ damaged units are discarded. The remaining $N - 6$ units are sold at $c + 20$ each, generating total profit $\$300$ (gross receipts $\$2,100$). Solve for original quantity $N$.

Problem 31 • Roundtrip Current Equation

A vessel travels $d = 36\text{ miles}$ upstream and returns downstream along a river with current $v_c = 3\text{ mph}$. Given total roundtrip time $t_{\text{total}} = 7\text{ h}$: $$\frac{36}{v_b - 3} + \frac{36}{v_b + 3} = 7$$ Calculate still-water cruising speed $v_b$.

Problem 32 • Sheet Fabrication Volume

An open box is constructed from a square metal sheet of side $x\text{ cm}$ by cutting $4\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm}$ corners and folding up sides. If box volume is $V = 4(x - 8)^2 = 576\text{ cm}^3$, determine initial sheet side length $x$.

Problem 33 • Freefall Acoustic Echo

A stone dropped into a canyon of depth $d$ strikes bottom at time $t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{d}{16}}$. Sound travels up at $v_s = 1100\text{ ft/s}$ taking $t_2 = \frac{d}{1100}$. Given total time $t_1 + t_2 = 5\text{ s}$: $$\sqrt{\frac{d}{16}} + \frac{d}{1100} = 5$$ Transform into a quadratic in $u = \sqrt{d}$ to calculate depth $d$.

Problem 34 • Rational Number Relation

The sum of a positive real number $x$ and $4$ times its reciprocal is $\frac{25}{3}$: $$x + \frac{4}{x} = \frac{25}{3}$$ Clear fractions to form $3x^2 - 25x + 12 = 0$ and solve for all valid roots $x$.

Problem 35 • Perpendicular Separation

Car $A$ travels North at velocity $v_A$. Car $B$ travels East at $v_B = v_A + 10$. After $t = 2\text{ hours}$, Euclidean distance between them is $D = 100\text{ miles}$: $$(2 v_A)^2 + (2(v_A + 10))^2 = 100^2$$ Calculate speeds $v_A$ and $v_B$.

Problem 36 • Rental Net Profit Optimization

A $100$-unit complex rents fully at $\$1,500/\text{month}$. Each $\$50$ rent increase causes $2$ vacancies ($N(x) = 100 - 2x$, $p(x) = 1500 + 50x$). Each occupied unit incurs $\$100/\text{month}$ maintenance. Maximize net profit $P(x) = N(x) \cdot (p(x) - 100)$.

Problem 37 • Geometric Mean Altitude

The altitude to the hypotenuse divides it into segments $x$ and $x+7$. By the Geometric Mean Theorem, $h^2 = x(x+7)$. Given altitude $h = 12\text{ cm}$: $$x(x + 7) = 144$$ Solve for $x$ and determine total hypotenuse length $c = 2x + 7$.

Problem 38 • Work Rate Schedule Shift

A construction team is contracted to lay $360\text{ m}^2$ of masonry in $D$ days. By laying $2\text{ m}^2/\text{day}$ extra ($r' = r + 2$), work completes $3$ days early ($D' = D - 3$): $$\frac{360}{r} - \frac{360}{r + 2} = 3$$ Solve for original daily output rate $r$.

Problem 39 • Vertical Clearance Interval

A tennis ball height is $h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 15t + 1$. Determine if it clears a barrier of height $h = 3.5\text{ m}$, and compute the precise duration $\Delta t = t_2 - t_1$ for which $h(t) \ge 3.5\text{ m}$.

Problem 40 • Shared Equipment Lease

A group of $n$ researchers lease a supercomputer cluster for $\$3,600/\text{month}$. Adding $3$ members reduces each member's monthly fee by $\$60$: $$\frac{3600}{n} - \frac{3600}{n + 3} = 60$$ Solve for original group size $n$.
