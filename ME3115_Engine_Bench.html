ME 3115 Engine Bench (free teaching model)

A simplified model built on the course formulas (cold-air constants, polytropic compression and expansion, constant-volume heat release, Chen–Flynn-type friction). It is not a validated simulator: use it to compare, tune and explain trends, and always cite your own engine data. Default boost and friction values are placeholders; replace them with cited values.

Compression P2=P1rnc; heat release at constant volume with cv=R/(khot−1); expansion P4=P3r−ne; W=Σ(PaVa−PbVb)/(n−1). imep=W/Vd−pmep; bmep=imep−fmep−(supercharger drive); &Wdot;b=bmep·Vd,totalN/2; bsfc=ṁf/&Wdot;b; ηb=1/(bsfc·qc).
